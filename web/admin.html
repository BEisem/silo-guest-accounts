How expiry works

While the plugin is running it checks every 5 minutes, and once more each time Silo runs the Guest accounts: expire accounts scheduled task. When an account's time is up it is disabled, which signs it out of every app and device straight away. Admin accounts and the Owner are never changed.

Turning an expired account back on by hand in Silo is respected: the plugin won't disable it again until you give it a new expiry.
